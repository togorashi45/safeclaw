# Content Atomizer

## Overview
Take a single piece of content (blog post, video transcript, deal breakdown) and repurpose it into 10-15 platform-specific pieces. Multiplies content output by 10x without 10x the work.

## When to Use

- You have a blog post, video, or deal breakdown to repurpose
- Need a week of social content from one piece
- Want to maximize reach across platforms

## Inputs
Provide relevant context: topic, audience, platform, deal details, brand guidelines as applicable.

## Process

### Step 1: Identify Core Content
Extract the 3-5 key takeaways, quotes, stats, and stories from the source content.

### Step 2: Platform Adaptation
For each platform, reformat:
- **Instagram**: Carousel (5-7 slides), Reel script (30-60s), Story series
- **Facebook**: Long-form post (300-500 words), shorter engagement post
- **LinkedIn**: Professional insight post, lesson learned format
- **TikTok/Reels**: Hook + value + CTA in 30-60 seconds
- **Email**: Newsletter section or standalone value email
- **Twitter/X**: Thread (5-8 tweets) or standalone tweet

### Step 3: Output All Variants
Provide complete copy for each platform, ready to post.

## Output Format
```
SOURCE: [Original content title]
PIECES GENERATED: [X]

INSTAGRAM:
  Carousel: [slide-by-slide copy]
  Reel Script: [script]
  Story: [3-5 frames]

FACEBOOK: [post copy]
LINKEDIN: [post copy]
TIKTOK: [script]
EMAIL: [email copy]
TWITTER: [thread]
```

## Example Prompts
- "Atomize this deal breakdown into posts for IG, FB, and LinkedIn: [paste content]"
- "I just recorded a 10-minute YouTube video on wholesaling. Break it into social content."
- "Turn this blog post into a week of social media content."
