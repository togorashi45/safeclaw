# Content Calendar Generator

## Overview
Plan an entire month of content in one session. Uses theme-weeks, content pillars (40% education, 25% proof, 20% personal, 10% engagement, 5% CTA), and platform-specific formatting.

## When to Use

- Starting a new month of content
- Content feels random or inconsistent
- Building presence across multiple platforms
- Batching content creation

## Inputs
Provide relevant context: topic, audience, platform, deal details, brand guidelines as applicable.

## Process

### Step 1: Set Theme Weeks
- Week 1: Education/Foundation
- Week 2: Proof/Results (deals, testimonials)
- Week 3: Behind the Scenes/Personal
- Week 4: Strategy Deep Dive + CTA

### Step 2: Platform Distribution
| Platform | Best Content | Frequency |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Instagram | Reels, carousels | 5x/week |
| Facebook | Long posts, video | 3x/week |
| LinkedIn | Thought leadership | 3-5x/week |
| YouTube | Tutorials, deal breakdowns | 1-2x/week |
| Email | Newsletter, value | 1-2x/week |

### Step 3: Generate Day-by-Day Calendar
For each day: platform, format, content idea/hook, content pillar.

### Step 4: Batch Day Workflow
Hour 1: Writing (captions, posts). Hour 2: Video (batch film). Hour 3: Schedule + review.

## Output Format
```
CONTENT CALENDAR — [Month]
| Day | Platform | Format | Hook/Idea | Pillar |
|-----|----------|--------|-----------|--------|
```

## Example Prompts
- "Create a 30-day content calendar for a wholesaler targeting motivated sellers. Instagram + Facebook + Email."
- "Plan next month's content. I flip houses in Dallas and want to attract private lenders."
