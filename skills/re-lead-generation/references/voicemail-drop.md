# Voicemail Drop Script Generator

## Overview
Create voicemail scripts optimized for ringless voicemail (RVM) drops. Short, personal, and designed to generate callbacks. Different scripts for different campaign types.

## When to Use
- Running a ringless voicemail campaign to a lead list
- Need voicemail scripts for cold calling no-answers
- Re-engaging old leads with a fresh voicemail
- A/B testing voicemail scripts for better callback rates

## Inputs
- Campaign type (cold outreach, warm follow-up, re-engagement, buyer outreach)
- Target list type (absentee, tax delinquent, expired, etc.)
- Your name and phone number
- Market/area
- Desired tone (casual, professional, urgent)

## Process

### Step 1: Script Guidelines
- Keep under 30 seconds (ideal: 18-22 seconds)
- Sound natural — like you're leaving a real voicemail, not reading
- Include your name, why you're calling, and a callback CTA
- Don't be salesy — be human and direct
- Include your phone number twice (beginning and end)

### Step 2: Generate Scripts by Type

**Cold Outreach:**
```
"Hey [First Name], this is [Your Name] — my number is [Number]. 
I'm a local real estate investor and I'm looking to buy a home 
in your area. Not sure if you'd be interested, but if you ever 
think about selling, I pay cash and close fast. 
Give me a call or text at [Number]. Thanks!"
```

**Warm Follow-Up:**
```
"Hey [First Name], it's [Your Name] again — [Number]. 
I reached out a little while ago about your property. 
Just wanted to circle back and see if anything's changed. 
No pressure at all — just give me a ring if you want to chat. 
[Number]. Have a good one."
```

**Re-Engagement (6+ months cold):**
```
"Hey [First Name], this is [Your Name] at [Number]. 
We talked a while back about your property — I wanted to 
let you know I'm still buying in the area and my offer 
might be different now. If you're curious, hit me back 
at [Number]. Take care."
```

**Urgency/Market Shift:**
```
"[First Name], it's [Your Name] — [Number]. Quick heads up — 
the market in your area has shifted and I'm adjusting my offers 
this month. If you've thought about selling, this might be 
good timing. Call or text [Number]."
```

### Step 3: A/B Test Variants
Generate 2-3 variants per script type for testing. Track callback rate per variant.

## Output Format
Complete voicemail script package with:
- 2-3 scripts per campaign type
- Recording tips
- Best send times (Tue-Thu, 10AM-12PM or 4PM-6PM)
- Expected callback rates (1-3% cold, 3-8% warm)
