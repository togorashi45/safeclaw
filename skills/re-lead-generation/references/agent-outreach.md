# Agent Outreach

## Overview
Real estate agents control access to pocket listings, pre-market deals, and expired listing intel. This skill builds systematic agent outreach campaigns to generate consistent off-market deal flow.

## When to Use
- Building a new agent referral network
- Looking for pocket listings in target areas
- Need off-market deal flow beyond direct-to-seller
- Want to position as the "easy buyer" agents call first

## Inputs
- Target market/zip codes
- Property types you buy
- Your buy criteria (price range, condition, deal types)
- Outreach channel preference (email, phone, in-person)

## Process

### Step 1: Identify Target Agents
Focus on agents who:
- List distressed or investor-priced properties
- Have expired listings in your target area
- Specialize in estate sales, probate, or foreclosures
- Are newer agents hungry for transactions (you close fast = they get paid fast)

### Step 2: Craft Outreach Message

**Email template:**
```
Subject: Cash Buyer in [Market] — Looking for Off-Market Deals

Hi [Agent Name],

I'm [Your Name], a local real estate investor actively buying 
properties in [area]. I buy with cash, close in 7-14 days, 
and I don't ask for repairs or credits.

If you ever have a listing that's been sitting, a pocket listing, 
or a seller who needs a fast close — I'd love to be your go-to buyer.

Here's my buy criteria:
- SFR and small MF in [zips/areas]
- $[min]-$[max] purchase price
- Any condition — the rougher the better

Happy to send proof of funds anytime. Would you be open to a 
quick call this week?

[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Email]
```

### Step 3: Follow-Up Cadence
| Touch | Timing | Channel |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Email intro |
| 2 | Day 3 | Phone call |
| 3 | Day 7 | Email with recent deal example |
| 4 | Day 14 | Text — "Hey [Name], did you get my email about buying in [area]?" |
| 5 | Day 30 | Email market update + reminder |
| Ongoing | Monthly | Value email (market data, comp report for their area) |

### Step 4: Track Relationships
Log each agent with: name, brokerage, area, last contact, deals sent, deals closed, relationship score (1-5).
