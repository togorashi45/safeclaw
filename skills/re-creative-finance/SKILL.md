# Creative Finance Playbooks

Subject-to, seller finance, wraps, notes, capital stacks, Dodd-Frank guardrails, balloon calendars, and JV structures.

25 playbooks. Do not load them all. Pick the closest play by its
description, read ONLY that file, then follow it. Pull live data from the
connected CRM, email, calendar, and the brain as the play directs.

## Plays

- `references/balloon-payment-calendar.md` - Track balloon payment dates across portfolio — alert thresholds, refinance planning, extension negotiation, and payoff calculations
- `references/borrower-workout-outreach.md` - Outreach templates for non-performing note borrowers — initial contact, modification offers, forbearance, and cash-for-keys
- `references/capital-stack.md` - Structure the capital stack for deals — debt/equity mix, senior/mezzanine tranches, cost of capital, OPM structuring, and investor terms
- `references/creative-deal-structurer.md` - Compare creative finance strategies side-by-side — sub-to vs seller finance vs wrap vs lease-option for any given deal
- `references/dodd-frank-dti.md` - Dodd-Frank compliance for seller financing — DTI calculation, ability-to-repay verification, exemption analysis, and safe harbor provisions
- `references/jv-split-tracker.md` - Track JV/partnership profit splits — equity contributions, preferred returns, waterfall structures, promote calculations, and distributions
- `references/lease-option.md` - Structure lease-option/rent-to-own deals — option fee, rent credit, purchase price, lease terms, and tenant-buyer qualification
- `references/lender-package.md` - Prepare lender/bank presentation packages — executive summary, property details, pro forma, borrower resume, and deal analysis
- `references/note-due-diligence.md` - Due diligence checklist for note purchases — 5-category DD across legal, collateral, borrower, payment history, and title. 15+ tracked items
- `references/note-investing-analyzer.md` - Analyze notes for purchase — yield calculation, ITV, collateral assessment, borrower analysis, and risk grading
- `references/note-portfolio-tracker.md` - Track portfolio of owned notes — payment log, UPB tracking, yield calculation, performance dashboard, event timeline, and watch list
- `references/note-sale-readiness.md` - Prepare notes for sale — loan file assembly, pricing at target yields, marketing package creation, and buyer channel identification
- `references/note-servicer-comparison.md` - Compare note servicing companies — fee structures, capabilities, reporting quality, compliance, borrower portal, and payment processing
- `references/note-tape-screener.md` - Screen and filter note tapes for acquisition targets — filter by UPB, ITV, state, performance status, interest rate, and remaining term. Score notes and flag re
- `references/note-tape-sourcing.md` - Source note tapes for acquisition — 10-channel source map, outreach templates, relationship building, and 90-day pipeline plan
- `references/note-workout-strategy.md` - Decision framework for non-performing notes — reinstatement, modification, forbearance, deed-in-lieu, cash-for-keys, foreclosure with side-by-side NPV compariso
- `references/note-yield-calculator.md` - Calculate note yields — IRR, yield-to-maturity, cash-on-cash, and total ROI including purchase discount and servicing costs
- `references/seller-finance-clauses.md` - Generate special clauses for seller finance contracts — due-on-sale, insurance requirements, tax escrow, property maintenance, and subordination
- `references/seller-finance-note-creator.md` - Draft promissory notes for seller-financed deals — terms, rate, payments, maturity, late fees, default provisions, acceleration, and prepayment
- `references/seller-finance-tape.md` - Aggregate seller finance notes into a standardized tape format for portfolio review or sale preparation
- `references/seller-finance-terms.md` - Structure seller finance offers — calculate payments at various rates/terms, balloon scenarios, amortization schedules, and compare to traditional financing
- `references/sub-to-analyzer.md` - Analyze subject-to deals — existing mortgage terms, equity capture, cash flow, DSCR, and due-on-sale risk assessment
- `references/wrap-mortgage-calculator.md` - Calculate wrap/AITD mortgage terms — underlying P&I, wrap rate, wrap payment, spread calculation, and monthly profit
- `references/wrap-note-default.md` - Default workflow for wrap mortgages — notice requirements, cure periods, underlying mortgage protection, and remedies
- `references/wrap-payment-manager.md` - Manage wrap mortgage payment flows — incoming wrap payment, outgoing underlying payment, spread capture, and escrow
