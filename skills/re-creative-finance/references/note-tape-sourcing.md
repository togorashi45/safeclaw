# Note Tape Sourcing

## Overview
Build a pipeline of note acquisition opportunities across 10 sourcing channels. Includes outreach templates for each channel, relationship-building strategies, and a 90-day ramp plan to get consistent deal flow. Notes don't come to you — you have to build the sourcing machine.

## When to Use
- Starting a note investing business — need deal flow
- Current tape sources have dried up — need new channels
- Want to diversify beyond one note seller
- Building a 90-day sourcing plan

## Inputs
- **Target note types**: performing, sub-performing, NPL, 1st lien, 2nd lien
- **Geographic focus**: states, regions
- **UPB range**: min/max
- **Capital available**: for acquisitions
- **Current sources**: what's already working (if any)

## Process

### Step 1: 10-Channel Source Map

| # | Channel | Note Types | Volume | Competition | Ease of Entry |
|---|---------|-----------|--------|------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Community banks/credit unions** | Performing, sub-perf | Low-Med | Low | Medium |
| 2 | **Note brokers/exchanges** | All types | High | High | Easy |
| 3 | **Hedge funds (portfolio sales)** | Large pools, NPL heavy | Very High | High | Hard |
| 4 | **Private sellers (owner-financed)** | Performing, seller-created | Low | Very Low | Medium |
| 5 | **Online note marketplaces** | All types | Medium | Medium | Easy |
| 6 | **FDIC/failed bank sales** | Mixed pools | High | Medium | Medium |
| 7 | **Bankruptcy trustees** | Distressed | Low | Low | Medium |
| 8 | **Mortgage servicers** | NPL, sub-perf | High | High | Hard |
| 9 | **REI networking/meetups** | Seller-created, small pools | Low | Low | Easy |
| 10 | **Direct to note holders (skip trace)** | Seller-created, private | Low | Very Low | Hard |

### Step 2: Outreach Templates by Channel

**Community Bank Template:**
```
Subject: Interest in Purchasing Non-Performing or Sub-Performing Notes

Dear [Bank Officer],
I'm a note investor specializing in [state] residential notes. I acquire
performing and non-performing 1st lien notes and can close quickly with
cash. If your institution has any notes you'd consider selling, I'd
welcome a conversation about your portfolio.

[Your credentials — assets under management, past purchases, references]
```

**Private Note Holder Template:**
```
Subject: Interested in Purchasing Your Seller-Financed Note

Dear [Name],
I understand you hold a promissory note secured by [property address].
I purchase real estate notes and can offer you a lump sum for your
remaining payments. Would you be interested in exploring a cash buyout?

No obligation — happy to provide a free quote.
```

### Step 3: 90-Day Pipeline Plan
| Week | Action | Target |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1-2 | Register on 3 note exchanges, join 2 note investing groups | Access to tape flow |
| 3-4 | Outreach to 10 community banks in target states | 2-3 conversations |
| 5-6 | Contact 5 note brokers, request sample tapes | 3+ tapes to screen |
| 7-8 | Skip trace 20 private note holders, send outreach | 3-5 responses |
| 9-10 | Submit bids on 5-10 notes from screened tapes | 1-2 accepted bids |
| 11-12 | Close first note, build repeat relationships | Pipeline established |

### Step 4: Relationship Management
- Track all contacts in CRM (GHL)
- Follow up with tape sources monthly
- Send proof of funds / credibility package to new sources
- Close deals quickly to build reputation as reliable buyer
- Ask every seller: "What else do you have?"
