# Seller Finance Clauses

## Overview
Generate protective clauses for seller finance transactions. Covers due-on-sale provisions, insurance requirements, tax/insurance escrow, property maintenance standards, subordination agreements, and other special terms that protect both parties.

## When to Use
- Drafting a seller finance agreement — need protective clauses
- Reviewing an existing note for missing protections
- Adding provisions to protect your position as note holder
- Structuring wrap mortgage protections

## Inputs
- **Your role** — note holder (seller) or borrower (buyer)
- **Deal type** — straight seller finance, wrap, sub-to with wrap
- **Specific clauses needed** or full review
- **Property and deal details**

## Process

### Step 1: Essential Clauses for Note Holders

| Clause | Purpose | Standard Language |
|--------|---------|-------------------|
| Insurance Requirement | Borrower must maintain hazard insurance with lender as additional insured | Min coverage = replacement cost |
| Tax Escrow | Monthly tax escrow to prevent tax lien priority | 1/12 annual taxes collected monthly |
| Property Maintenance | Property must be maintained in current or better condition | Annual inspection right |
| Due on Sale | Acceleration if property transferred without consent | Standard in most notes |
| Late Payment | Define grace period and late fee | 10-15 day grace, 5% late fee |
| Default Notice | Cure period before acceleration | 30-day cure period typical |
| Acceleration | Right to demand full balance on default | Triggered by material default |
| Prepayment | Allow/restrict early payoff | With or without penalty |
| Subordination | Position relative to other liens | Non-subordination clause protects position |

### Step 2: Protective Clauses for Borrowers
- No prepayment penalty (or limited)
- Right to cure default within specified period
- Written notice required before acceleration
- Right of first refusal if note is sold
- Assumption rights

### Step 3: Wrap-Specific Clauses
- Underlying loan payment verification
- Borrower's right to verify underlying payments
- Protection if underlying loan is called
- Payment application order (underlying first)
