# Note Servicer Comparison

## Overview
Evaluate and compare note servicing companies for your portfolio. Compares fees, capabilities, reporting quality, compliance handling, borrower communication, and payment processing. The right servicer protects your investment; the wrong one creates liability.

## When to Use
- Just acquired your first note — need a servicer
- Unhappy with current servicer — evaluating alternatives
- Portfolio growing — need a servicer that scales
- Acquiring NPLs — need a servicer with workout capabilities

## Inputs
- **Portfolio size**: number of notes, total UPB
- **Note types**: performing, NPL, modified, seller-financed
- **Required capabilities**: payment processing, escrow, default management, loss mitigation
- **Geographic focus**: states where your notes are secured
- **Current servicer** (if switching): what's not working

## Process

### Step 1: Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | Weight | What to Look For |
|----------|--------|-----------------|
| **Setup/boarding fee** | 10% | $0-300 per loan; some waive for volume |
| **Monthly servicing fee** | 20% | $15-50/loan performing; $25-75 NPL |
| **Payment processing** | 15% | ACH, check, online portal, mobile |
| **Borrower portal** | 10% | Self-service payment, statements, docs |
| **Reporting** | 15% | Monthly statements, custom reports, data export |
| **Default management** | 10% | Collections, workout capability, foreclosure coordination |
| **Compliance** | 10% | CFPB, state licensing, SCRA, bankruptcy |
| **Escrow management** | 5% | Tax and insurance escrow capability |
| **Customer service** | 5% | Response time, dedicated rep, hours |

### Step 4: Red Flags When Evaluating Servicers
- Not licensed in your note's state
- No borrower-facing portal or payment options
- Cannot handle loss mitigation or modifications
- Poor compliance track record (check CFPB complaints)
- Long boarding times (>30 days)
- No ability to handle escrow
- Requires large minimum portfolio size
