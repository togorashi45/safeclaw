# Cold Outreach (ninja targets)

This is not a blast skill. It is the opposite: find the FEW targets worth a
personal, sequential, multi-channel pursuit and run that pursuit relentlessly
with the client in the loop. Volume outreach belongs to the CRM campaigns;
this skill is for the deals that pay for the year.

## Phase 1 - Find the ninja targets
Sweep everything the agent can reach and score candidates:
- **CRM**: high-value opportunities gone cold, big monetary values, repeat
  engagers who never converted.
- **LeadForge / prospecting data** (when linked): top signal-quality
  prospects - distress signals stacked on high-equity or high-value parcels.
- **The second brain**: past meetings, emails, and notes that mention an
  owner, a building, or a seller the client wanted; internal databases the
  brain indexes.
- **Social**: the target's public presence (who they are, what they post,
  mutual context worth referencing).
Score on: deal size, signal strength, reachability (channels we actually
have for them), and fit with the client's buy box. Present the top 5-10 to
the client with one-line whys. **The client picks the targets** - never
start a campaign unpicked.

## Phase 2 - Dossier per target
One page per target before any touch: who they are, the property/company,
every phone/email/address/social handle we hold and where it came from
(provenance matters), relationship history, the angle (why us, why now), and
the opening message that does not sound like marketing.

## Phase 3 - Sequential campaign
Default cadence (adapt per target, log every touch to the CRM + brain):
- Day 1: social touch (follow/comment/DM draft) - warm, human, references
  something real.
- Day 2: text draft.
- Day 4: cold call - brief the client with a call sheet (opener, angle,
  objections), or place via the AI caller only if the client has one
  approved and configured.
- Day 6: email draft.
- Day 9: direct mail piece - generate the letter/postcard copy for the
  client's mail flow.
- Day 12: reminder task + calendar block for the client to CALL or
  DOOR-KNOCK personally; include the dossier in the reminder.
- Then: 2-week pulse until reply, dead, or client stops it.
On ANY reply: stop the sequence, alert the client immediately with the
thread + suggested response draft.

## Hard rules (non-negotiable)
- **Every outbound message on every channel is a DRAFT the client approves.**
  No auto-send: not texts, not DMs, not emails, not calls. Reminders and
  internal tasks are the only self-executing steps.
- **Compliance before channel:** scrub DNC/litigator lists before any text or
  call; honor opt-outs across ALL channels immediately; no texting numbers
  without a compliant basis. If the client lacks a scrub tool, say so and
  hold that channel.
- One target, one owner, one thread: everything logs to the CRM contact and
  the brain so the next session picks up mid-pursuit.
- Data provenance: use only data the client's own systems hold or public
  sources; never buy data inside this skill.

`references/cadence.md` holds the editable default cadence and per-channel
message patterns.
