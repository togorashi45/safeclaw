# Operations Playbooks

Daily planning, calendar optimization, meeting prep, and back-office operations for an investing business.

9 playbooks. Do not load them all. Pick the closest play by its
description, read ONLY that file, then follow it. Pull live data from the
connected CRM, email, calendar, and the brain as the play directs.

## Plays

- `references/calendar-optimizer.md` - Optimize daily and weekly calendar — time-block lead gen, acquisition calls, deal analysis, admin, and property visits
- `references/crm-sync.md` - CRM cleanup and sync — identify duplicates, stale leads, missing follow-ups, incomplete records, and pipeline issues. GHL is source of truth
- `references/daily-briefing.md` - Morning briefing — deals needing action, follow-ups due, appointments, closings this week, and urgent items from all sources
- `references/daily-planner.md` - Plan the day using Eisenhower matrix — prioritize by urgency and importance, block time for deep work and calls
- `references/email-triage.md` - Triage inbox — categorize emails by deals, leads, tenants, marketing, and financial. Flag urgent items and draft routine responses
- `references/meeting-notes.md` - Process meeting notes and transcripts — extract action items, decisions, follow-ups, key numbers, and next meeting date
- `references/meeting-prep.md` - Prepare for meetings — pull context on who you are meeting, deal history, property details, talking points, and documents needed
- `references/task-prioritizer.md` - Stack rank tasks by ROI and urgency — score by revenue impact, time sensitivity, and effort. Output prioritized task list
- `references/weekly-review.md` - End of week retrospective — deals progressed, deals stalled, KPIs, marketing results, money in and out, wins, and lessons
