# Meeting Prep

## Overview
Prepare a briefing document for any upcoming meeting. Pulls context on the person/company, relevant deal history, property details, talking points, and documents to have ready. Ensures you walk in informed and prepared.

## When to Use
- Before a seller appointment
- Before a buyer meeting
- Before an investor/partner meeting
- Before a team meeting or 1-on-1
- Before a networking meeting or mastermind

## Inputs
- **Meeting details**: who, when, where, purpose (from Google Calendar)
- **Contact history**: from CRM (GHL), prior emails, prior meetings
- **Deal context**: any properties or deals associated with this contact
- **Meeting goal**: what outcome do you want from this meeting?

## Process

### Step 1: Contact Intelligence
```
MEETING WITH: [Name]
COMPANY: [If applicable]
ROLE: [Seller / Buyer / Agent / Investor / Partner / Team]
PHONE: [Number]
EMAIL: [Email]

RELATIONSHIP HISTORY:
- First contact: [Date] via [Source]
- Last contact: [Date] — [What was discussed]
- Total interactions: [X]
- Notes: [Key details about this person]
```

### Step 2: Deal Context (if applicable)
```
RELATED DEAL: [Property address]
Stage: [Current stage]
Key Numbers: ARV $[X] | Offer $[X] | Rehab $[X]
Outstanding Issues: [What needs resolution]
```

### Step 3: Meeting Agenda & Talking Points
```
MEETING GOAL: [What you want to achieve]

AGENDA:
1. [Opening / rapport] — 5 min
2. [Main topic 1] — [X] min
3. [Main topic 2] — [X] min
4. [Next steps / close] — 5 min

TALKING POINTS:
- [Key point to make]
- [Question to ask]
- [Number to reference]
- [Objection to prepare for]
```

### Step 4: Documents Needed
- [ ] [Contract / offer letter / comps / etc.]
- [ ] [Proof of funds if needed]
- [ ] [Property photos / inspection report]

## Output Format
```
MEETING BRIEF — [Person] | [Date/Time]
═══════════════════════════════════════
[Contact intelligence]
[Deal context]
[Agenda and talking points]
[Documents checklist]
```

## Example Prompts
- "Prep me for my 2pm meeting with John Smith — we're discussing the 123 Main St deal."
- "Who am I meeting today and what do I need to know?"
- "Prepare a meeting brief for my call with the title company about the Oak Ave closing."
