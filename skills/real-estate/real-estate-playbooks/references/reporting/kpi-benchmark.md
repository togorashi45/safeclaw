# KPI Benchmark

## Overview
Compare your business KPIs against industry benchmarks for RE investors. Covers wholesaling, flipping, rental, and note investing benchmarks. Identifies where you're above/below standard and recommends focus areas.

## When to Use
- Evaluating business performance against peers
- Setting realistic targets for next quarter
- Identifying areas of competitive advantage or weakness
- Preparing for investor conversations

## Inputs
- **Your KPIs** (from /reporting/kpi-tracker or manual input)
- **Business type**: wholesale, flip, rental, note, or hybrid
- **Market**: hot, balanced, or cool
- **Stage**: startup (<1 yr), growth (1-3 yr), established (3+ yr)

## Process

### Step 1: Wholesaling Benchmarks

| KPI | Your # | Startup | Growth | Top Performer |
|-----|--------|---------|--------|---------------|
| Deals/month | [X] | 1-2 | 3-5 | 8-15+ |
| Avg assignment fee | $[X] | $5-8K | $8-15K | $15-25K+ |
| Cost per lead | $[X] | $30-80 | $20-50 | $10-30 |
| Cost per deal | $[X] | $3-8K | $2-5K | $1-3K |
| Marketing ROI | [X]x | 2-4x | 4-8x | 8-15x |
| Lead-to-close rate | [X]% | 1-2% | 2-4% | 4-7% |
| Avg days to close | [X] | 30-60 | 21-45 | 14-30 |
| Gross margin | [X]% | 40-60% | 55-70% | 65-80% |

### Step 2: Flipping Benchmarks

| KPI | Your # | Startup | Growth | Top Performer |
|-----|--------|---------|--------|---------------|

| Budget accuracy | [X]% | +/- 20% | +/- 10% | +/- 5% |

### Step 3: Rental Portfolio Benchmarks

| KPI | Your # | Startup | Growth | Top Performer |
|-----|--------|---------|--------|---------------|
| Occupancy rate | [X]% | 85-90% | 90-95% | 95-98% |
| Rent collection rate | [X]% | 90-95% | 95-97% | 97-99% |

| Expense ratio | [X]% | 50-60% | 40-50% | 35-45% |
| Cap rate | [X]% | varies by market | varies | varies |
| Avg turnover cost | $[X] | $2-4K | $1.5-3K | $1-2K |

### Step 4: Scoring
For each KPI: compare your number to the benchmark range.
- **Above Top Performer** = Exceptional (keep doing this)
- **Growth to Top** = Strong (optimize further)
- **Startup to Growth** = Average (room for improvement)
- **Below Startup** = Underperforming (needs attention)
