# Fair Housing Compliance

## Overview
Review your marketing, listings, tenant communications, and screening processes for Fair Housing Act compliance. Ensures you're not inadvertently violating federal, state, or local fair housing laws that carry severe penalties ($16K-150K+ per violation).

## When to Use
- Writing property listings or rental ads
- Screening tenant applications — need to verify criteria is lawful
- Responding to tenant complaints about discrimination
- Training VAs or team members on compliance
- Reviewing marketing materials before publishing

## Inputs
- **Material to review** — listing text, ad copy, screening criteria, communication
- **Property type** — rental, sale, or both
- **Location** — state/city (some have additional protected classes)
- **Context** — what triggered the review

## Process

### Step 1: Federal Protected Classes (Fair Housing Act)
| Protected Class | Cannot Discriminate Based On |
|----------------|------------------------------|
| Race | Race, color |
| Religion | Religious beliefs or practices |
| National Origin | Country of origin, ethnicity, accent |
| Sex | Gender, sexual harassment |
| Familial Status | Children under 18, pregnancy, custody |
| Disability | Physical or mental disability |
| Color | Skin color |

**Additional classes in many states/cities:** Sexual orientation, gender identity, source of income (Section 8), age, marital status, veteran status, criminal history (ban-the-box)

### Step 2: Advertising Compliance Review
**Words/Phrases to AVOID:**
- "Perfect for young professionals" (age, familial status)
- "Great for singles" (familial status)
- "Walking distance to [church/mosque]" (religion)
- "Quiet neighborhood" (can imply no children)
- "No Section 8" (source of income — illegal in some jurisdictions)
- "English speakers only" (national origin)
- "No wheelchairs" (disability)
- Descriptions of neighbors' demographics

**Safe Alternative Language:**
- Describe the PROPERTY, not the ideal TENANT
- "2BR/1BA, updated kitchen, fenced yard, near schools and parks"
- Focus on features, amenities, location attributes

### Step 3: Screening Criteria Review
**Lawful Criteria (apply uniformly):**
- Credit score minimum (same for all applicants)
- Income requirement (e.g., 3x rent — same for all)
- Rental history verification
- Employment verification
- Criminal background (where permitted — check local laws)

**Unlawful Criteria:**
- Any protected class as a factor
- Subjective "gut feeling" decisions
- Different standards for different applicants
- Blanket criminal history bans (many jurisdictions)

### Step 4: Reasonable Accommodations (Disability)
- Must allow service animals and emotional support animals (even in no-pet properties)
- Must allow reasonable modifications (ramps, grab bars) at tenant's expense
- Must make reasonable accommodations to rules/policies
- Cannot charge pet deposit for service/ESA animals

### Step 5: Documentation Best Practices
- [ ] Uniform screening criteria documented in writing
- [ ] All applicants given same application
- [ ] Rejection reasons documented (based on criteria, not protected class)
- [ ] All communications professional and neutral
- [ ] Records retained for 3+ years
- [ ] Fair housing poster displayed at office/property
