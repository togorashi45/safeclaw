# TREC Contract Guide

## Overview
Walk through the Texas Real Estate Commission (TREC) 1-4 Family Residential Contract (Resale) section by section with investor-specific guidance. Covers option period strategy, earnest money, as-is language, and common investor modifications using TREC addenda.

## When to Use
- Buying or selling a 1-4 unit residential property in Texas
- Need guidance on which TREC paragraphs to focus on
- Structuring option period and earnest money for wholesale/flip deals
- Determining which TREC addenda are needed

## Inputs
- **Deal type** — wholesale, flip acquisition, rental acquisition, retail sale
- **Property address**
- **Purchase price**
- **Option fee and period** desired
- **Earnest money** amount
- **Financing type** — cash, conventional, FHA/VA, seller finance
- **Closing date** target
- **Special circumstances** — tenants, repairs, personal property

## Process

### Step 1: TREC Form Selection
| Situation | Form |
|-----------|------|
| Standard 1-4 family resale | TREC 20-17 (One to Four Family Residential Contract) |
| New construction | TREC 23-18 |
| Farm/ranch | TREC 25-15 |
| Unimproved land | TREC 26-8 |
| Condo | TREC 30-14 |

### Step 2: Key Paragraphs for Investors

| Paragraph | What It Covers | Investor Strategy |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **1 - Parties** | Buyer/Seller names | Use "and/or assigns" for wholesale |
| **3 - Sales Price** | Price and financing | Cash = fastest close, strongest offer |
| **4 - License Holder** | Agent disclosure | Disclose if you have a license |
| **5 - Earnest Money** | EMD amount and escrow | Keep low for wholesale ($100-500), higher for competitive offers |
| **6 - Title** | Title policy, survey | Seller typically pays title policy in TX |
| **7 - Property Condition** | As-is vs repairs | Check "as-is" (7D) for investor deals |
| **8 - Broker Fees** | Commission | N/A in direct-to-seller deals |
| **11 - Special Provisions** | Custom terms | Add assignability, extended close, etc. |
| **23 - Option** | Option fee and period | $10-100 fee, 7-14 days for wholesale; longer for flips |

### Step 3: Option Period Strategy
```
Wholesale:  $10-50 option fee, 7-10 day option period
            (Keep fee low — it's non-refundable)
Flip:       $100-500 option fee, 10-21 day option period
            (Need time for thorough inspection and contractor bids)
Rental:     $100-500 option fee, 14-21 day option period
            (Need time for rent comps, tenant estoppel, inspection)
```

### Step 4: Common TREC Addenda for Investors
- **Addendum for Back-Up Contract** — When you're backup offer
- **Addendum for Property Subject to Mandatory Membership** — HOA properties
- **Seller Financing Addendum** — When seller is carrying the note
- **Third Party Financing Addendum** — Bank/hard money financing
- **Addendum for Sale of Other Property** — If contingent on selling another property
- **Short Sale Addendum** — Bank-owned/short sale properties

### Step 5: Special Provisions (Paragraph 11) — Investor Language
Common additions:
- "Buyer and/or assigns" right to assign
- Extended closing date with written notice
- Access for inspections, contractors, appraisers prior to closing
- Seller to provide all leases, rent rolls, and tenant information
- Personal property included (appliances, fixtures list)
