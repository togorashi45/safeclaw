# Content Ideation Engine

## Overview
Brainstorm 30+ content ideas tailored to your RE niche, audience, and platforms. Never run out of things to post.

## When to Use

- Drawing a blank on what to post
- Need a batch of ideas for the month
- Want to diversify content types

## Inputs
Provide relevant context: topic, audience, platform, deal details, brand guidelines as applicable.

## Process

### Step 1: Define Audience and Niche
Who are you talking to? (Sellers, investors, students, agents, lenders)

### Step 2: Generate Ideas by Pillar
**Education (12 ideas):** How-tos, strategy explainers, mistakes to avoid, market education.
**Proof/Results (8 ideas):** Deal breakdowns, before/after, testimonials, check screenshots.
**Personal (6 ideas):** Day in the life, origin story, lessons learned, workspace tour.
**Engagement (4 ideas):** Polls, questions, hot takes, fill-in-the-blank.

### Step 3: Format Ideas with Hooks
Each idea includes a hook headline that stops the scroll.

## Example Prompts
- "Give me 30 content ideas for a wholesaler in Dallas. Mix of IG, YouTube, and email."
- "What should I post about this week? I just closed a flip and have a new deal under contract."
