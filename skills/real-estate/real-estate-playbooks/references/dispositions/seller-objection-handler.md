# Seller Objection Handler

## Overview
Proven responses to every common seller objection. Not about manipulation — about understanding their concern and presenting your value clearly.

## When to Use
- Seller pushes back on your offer
- Need to overcome a specific objection during negotiation
- Training team on objection handling
- Preparing for a seller meeting

## Inputs
- The specific objection (or ask for the full playbook)
- Deal context (property, offer, seller situation)

## Process

### Objection Response Library

| Objection | Response Strategy |
|-----------|------------------|
| "Your offer is too low" | Explain the math: ARV, rehab, costs, your profit margin. Show the numbers. "Here's how I arrived at this number..." |
| "I want to list it with an agent" | "That's a great option. The trade-off is you'll pay 5-6% commission, make repairs, show the house for weeks/months, and may deal with buyer financing falling through. My offer saves you all of that. What would you net after agent fees and repairs?" |
| "I need to think about it" | "Absolutely, take your time. What specifically do you want to think through? Maybe I can provide more info to help." Set a follow-up date. |
| "I'm not in a hurry" | "That's fine — no rush. Out of curiosity, what would make you want to move forward? Is it price, timing, or something else?" |
| "I got a higher offer" | "That's great — you should take the best deal. Just to make sure you're comparing apples to apples: is their offer cash? What's their timeline? Any contingencies?" |
| "I want full market value" | "I understand. Full market means listing, repairs, showings, and 60-90 day process. My offer reflects the convenience of selling as-is in [X] days. What matters more to you — top dollar or speed and certainty?" |
| "How do I know you're legit?" | Provide: proof of funds, references, recent deal examples, company info, Google reviews. "Happy to provide anything that gives you confidence." |

### Step 2: Context-Specific Response
Tailor the response to the seller's specific situation (divorce, foreclosure, estate, tired landlord).

## Output Format
Specific response script tailored to the objection and deal context, plus 2-3 follow-up questions to keep the conversation moving.

## Example Prompts
- "The seller said my offer of $95K is too low on a $185K ARV property. Handle this objection."
- "Seller wants to think about it — they've been saying that for 2 weeks. What do I say?"
- "Give me the full objection handler playbook for my next seller call."
