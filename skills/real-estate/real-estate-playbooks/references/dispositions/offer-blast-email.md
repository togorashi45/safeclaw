# Offer Blast Email Generator

## Overview
Create deal blast emails optimized for buyer response. Includes subject line variants, email body, follow-up bump, and SMS version for multi-channel distribution.

## When to Use
- Deal under contract, need to move it to a buyer
- Re-blasting a deal with updated terms or price drop
- Sending a "coming soon" teaser to build buzz
- Building buyer list engagement with deal flow

## Inputs
- Property address, beds/baths/sqft/lot
- ARV, asking price, estimated rehab
- Projected buyer profit
- Closing deadline
- Property photos or description
- Unique selling points

## Process

### Step 1: Generate 3 Subject Lines
- Curiosity: "You'll want to see this one — [City]"
- Profit: "$[X]K profit in [City] — details inside"
- Urgency: "Closes [Day] — [Address] deal brief"

### Step 2: Write Email Body
Structure: Hook → Deal Brief (bullets) → The Math → Scarcity → CTA

### Step 3: Follow-Up Bump (24-48h)
Shorter, more urgent. One new piece of info.

### Step 4: SMS Version (<160 chars)

## Output Format
Complete package: 3 subject lines, email body, follow-up email, SMS version.

## Example Prompts
- "Write a deal blast: 3/2 SFR in 75217 Dallas. ARV $185K, asking $95K, ~$35K rehab. Close by March 31."
- "Re-blast 456 Oak Ave with a price drop from $95K to $88K. Make it urgent."
- "Write a 'coming soon' teaser for a deal I'm locking up Friday in South Dallas."
