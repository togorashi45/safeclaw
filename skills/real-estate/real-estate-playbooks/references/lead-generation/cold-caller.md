# Cold Call Script Generator

## Overview
Generate effective cold call scripts tailored to specific seller situations — absentee owners, tax delinquent, pre-foreclosure, expired listings. Includes qualification questions, objection handling, and appointment booking.

## When to Use
- Starting a new cold calling campaign
- Training a VA or caller on your script
- Targeting a specific list type that needs a custom approach
- Current script isn't converting — need a refresh

## Inputs
- List type (absentee, tax delinquent, pre-foreclosure, expired, probate, high equity)
- Your name and company
- Market/area
- Call objective (appointment, gauge interest, make verbal offer)
- Tone preference (friendly/casual, professional, urgent)

## Process

### Step 1: Generate Opening Script
Keep it under 15 seconds. Be human, not salesy.

**Universal opener:**
```
"Hi, is this [First Name]? Hey [First Name], this is [Your Name]. 
I'm a local real estate investor — I was reaching out because 
I'm looking to buy a property in [area]. I'm not sure if it's 
something you'd consider, but would you be open to hearing a 
quick cash offer on [address]?"
```

### Step 2: Qualification Questions (If They're Open)
Ask in this order — stop if they're clearly not interested:
1. "What's your situation with the property?" (motivation)
2. "Have you thought about what you'd want for it?" (price expectation)
3. "If we could agree on a fair price, how soon would you want to close?" (timeline)
4. "Is the property currently occupied?" (occupancy)
5. "What kind of condition is it in?" (rehab level)
6. "Do you have a mortgage on it, or is it free and clear?" (equity)

### Step 3: Objection Handling Scripts

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I'm not interested" | "Totally understand. Mind if I ask — is it the timing, or you just don't want to sell? Because if it's timing, I'm happy to check back in a few months." |
| "How'd you get my number?" | "Public records — I research properties in [area] and yours came up. I apologize if this caught you off guard." |
| "What would you offer?" | "I'd need to look at it first, but I typically pay fair cash prices based on condition. Could I come take a quick look this week?" |
| "I want full market value" | "I totally get that. The trade-off with us is speed and convenience — no repairs, no agents, no fees, close in 7-14 days. For some people that's worth it, for others it's not. Would you be open to seeing a number?" |
| "I'm working with an agent" | "No problem at all. If it doesn't sell or the listing expires, feel free to give me a call. I'll leave you my number." |
| "Send me something in writing" | "Absolutely. What's the best email? I'll send over some info today." → Route to email follow-up |

### Step 4: Appointment Close
```
"Based on what you're telling me, I think we could make something work. 
Would it be easier to meet at the property Tuesday or Thursday? 
I just need about 15 minutes to walk through and I can have 
an offer for you same day."
```

## Output Format
Full script document with:
- Opening (2 versions)
- Qualification flow
- Objection handlers (6-8 common objections)
- Appointment close
- Voicemail script (if no answer)
- Notes for caller
