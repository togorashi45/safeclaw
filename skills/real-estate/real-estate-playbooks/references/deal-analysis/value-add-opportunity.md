# Value-Add Opportunity Finder

## Overview
Find the hidden profit in a property by identifying value-add strategies. Every $1 of NOI increase at a 7% cap rate adds ~$14 to property value.

## When to Use
- Analyzing a potential acquisition for upside
- Planning renovations on an existing property
- Preparing a business plan for lenders/investors
- Maximizing exit price before selling

## Inputs
- Property details and current financials
- Current rent roll vs market rents
- Current expense breakdown
- Physical condition assessment
- Zoning information

## Process

### Step 1: Revenue Enhancement
- Below-market rents → raise to market after improvements
- Add utility billing (RUBS — Ratio Utility Billing System)
- Add ancillary income (laundry, parking, storage, pet fees, vending)
- Reduce vacancy with better marketing/management
- Convert underutilized space (garage to ADU, add units)

### Step 2: Expense Reduction
- Challenge property tax assessment
- Shop insurance annually
- Reduce utility costs (LED, low-flow, smart thermostats)
- Self-manage or renegotiate management fee
- Preventive maintenance vs reactive

### Step 3: Physical Improvements
- Interior updates (paint, flooring, fixtures, appliances)
- Curb appeal (landscaping, exterior paint, signage)
- Unit additions (ADU, convert common areas)
- Energy efficiency upgrades (HVAC, insulation, windows)

### Step 4: Calculate Value Impact
For each opportunity: estimate cost, NOI impact, and value creation.
