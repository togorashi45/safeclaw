# Offer Letter Drip

## Overview
Send a sequence of written offers to sellers, each with a slightly different angle or adjusted terms. Works well for off-market outreach where you want to be persistent without being pushy.

## When to Use
- Sending initial written offer to a seller
- Follow-up offers after no response
- Adjusting terms to make the deal work
- Bulk offer campaigns to a targeted list

## Inputs
- Seller name and property address
- Your offer price and terms
- Property details and ARV
- Number of touches (default: 3 over 30 days)

## Process

### Step 1: Initial Offer (Day 0)
Professional letter with specific offer amount, key terms, and clear CTA.

### Step 2: Follow-Up #1 (Day 10)
Same offer, different angle — emphasize convenience, speed, or a specific pain point.

### Step 3: Follow-Up #2 (Day 21)
Slight urgency — "offer is open for 10 more days" or adjust terms slightly.

### Step 4: Final Touch (Day 30)
Last attempt — "this is my final offer at these terms" or slight increase.

## Output Format
Complete 3-4 letter sequence with timing, each letter fully drafted.

## Example Prompts
- "Write a 3-touch offer letter sequence for the seller at 123 Main St. My offer is $95K cash, close in 14 days."
- "Draft a follow-up offer to a seller who didn't respond to my first letter 2 weeks ago."
