# KPI Tracker

## Overview
Track the key performance indicators that drive your RE investment business. Monitors the full funnel from leads to closed deals, plus marketing efficiency, financial performance, and operational metrics. Shows trend arrows so you know if you're improving or declining.

## When to Use
- Weekly or monthly performance review
- Identifying bottlenecks in the deal funnel
- Evaluating marketing channel effectiveness
- Setting targets for next period

## Inputs
- **Time period** (week, month, quarter)
- **Lead data**: leads by source, appointments, offers
- **Deal data**: contracts signed, deals closed, revenue
- **Marketing spend**: by channel
- **Pipeline data**: from mind/active-deals.md
