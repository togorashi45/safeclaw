# Financial Q&A

## Overview
Answer RE investor financial and accounting questions with practical, actionable guidance. Covers depreciation methods, 1031 exchanges, cost segregation studies, entity structuring, self-directed IRAs, capital gains strategies, and general RE tax/accounting topics. Not tax advice — educational guidance to discuss with your CPA.

## When to Use
- Quick financial/accounting question
- Need to understand a tax strategy before talking to CPA
- Evaluating a financial decision (entity election, depreciation method, etc.)
- Learning about a new RE financial concept

## Common Topic Areas

### Depreciation
- Residential rental: 27.5 years straight-line
- Commercial: 39 years straight-line
- Cost segregation: reclassify components to 5, 7, 15-year life
- Bonus depreciation: 40% in 2026 (stepping down annually)
- Section 179: deduct qualifying assets fully in year 1

### 1031 Exchanges
- 45-day identification period, 180-day close
- Like-kind: any RE for any RE (investment/business use)
- Must use Qualified Intermediary (QI)
- Boot = taxable (cash or debt relief received)
- Reverse exchange: buy replacement before selling relinquished

### Capital Gains
- Short-term (<1 yr): ordinary income rates
- Long-term (>1 yr): 0/15/20% + 3.8% NIIT
- Depreciation recapture: 25% rate
- Installment sale: spread gains over payment years
- Opportunity Zones: defer and reduce gains

### Entity Structure
- SMLLC: simplest, no asset protection vs owner negligence
- Series LLC: separate liability per property (state-dependent)
- S-Corp: save SE tax on reasonable salary + distributions
- C-Corp: rarely used for RE (double taxation)
- Land trust: privacy, not tax benefits

### Self-Directed IRA / Solo 401(k)
- Buy RE with retirement funds
- All income/expenses must flow through the account
- Cannot do personal labor on IRA-owned properties
- UBIT on debt-financed income in IRA
- Solo 401(k) allows loans to self

## Process

### Step 1: Understand the Question
Clarify the specific scenario, entity type, and what decision they're trying to make.

### Step 2: Provide Educational Framework
Explain the relevant tax/financial concept with RE-specific context and examples.

### Step 3: Quantify When Possible
Show the dollar impact of different approaches.

### Step 4: CPA Handoff
Flag anything that requires CPA review before implementing.
